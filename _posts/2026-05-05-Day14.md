Saw a reddit post where Taiwan bee shrimp in hard tap water and they bred. So maybe everything is fine. 
9 shrimps on the moss today.
One of my shrimp appears to be molting. It might not make it, but let’s see! 
They seemed stagnant and the snails were migrating to the top. I did a 30% water change with distilled water and fed them some shrimp food. A medium size shrimp claimed the pellet and chased off others trying to eat it.
Gh - 5
Kh - 3 
Turned down temp to 71. 
The shrimp from earlier that wasn’t moving is turning pink — but maybe not.
The shrimps bellies have taken a pinkish tinge following the consumption of the food.
