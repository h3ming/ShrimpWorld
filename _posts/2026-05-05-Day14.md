Saw a reddit post where someone said they kept Taiwan bee shrimp in hard tap water and they bred. So maybe everything is fine. 

9 shrimps on the moss today. 

{% include slideshow.html photos=page.photos %}

The shrimp seemed a bit stagnant and the snails were migrating to the top. I did a 30% water change with distilled water I got at the CVS down the block. One of the weak stems uprooted again and I decided to remove it from the tank. 

{% include slideshow.html photos=page.plant %}

I tested the hardness again:

#### Tank  
`Gh - 5`  
`Kh - 3`

This is so livable for the shrimp now!!!!!! 
Turned down temp to 71 to be more comfortable for them. 73 was on their upper range.

I fed them shrimp food for the first time. The moment it fell, the shrimp immediately tore into it. A medium size shrimp claimed the pellet and chased off others trying to eat it, but it could only cover so much ground. 

{% include slideshow.html photos=page.feeding %}

The shrimps bellies have taken a pinkish tinge following the consumption of the food.
