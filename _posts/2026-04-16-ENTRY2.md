On the way back from class, I visit Target to check out my local soil options. Organic and non fertilized is what I’m looking for, and I do find one that seems like it should work. Though a bit more expensive than I would have liked, it was good to know I had something nearby to fall back on. 

I’ve looked at pretty much every aquatic plant listing in the Chicago area on FB marketplace at this point and my options are rather meager. ~~~It doesn’t make much sense for me to be running all over town for a few trimmings here and there, especially when I (don’t know what plants I want/need/are available).~~~ 

I find a somewhat recent posting on the r/ChicagoAquaSwap community on Reddit. It’s crossposted from the larger r/Aquaswap subreddit and features a list of latin names that I don’t recognize, but the title says “easy beginner plant bundle” and the price seems reasonable. Everything from the post is already marked as sold, so I leave a comment asking if they have any stock available for this weekend. 

They get back to me within 10 minutes with a list of available plants. After some googling, I let them know I’m interested in everything aside from the creeping Jenny (for reasons unknown. Genuinely don’t remember why). We make plans to pickup the following Monday evening. 

I finally pull the trigger on an order of siphon tube pump, cheap water testing strips, some long tweezers and curved scissors, and water conditioner off Amazon.
