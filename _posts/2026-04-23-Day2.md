14 salvinia. 
SNAIL DISCOVERY ! I discover another smaller one later in the day. I also saw some small white worms that Google and Reddit say are likely detritus worms. The red stem plant in the middle seems to be doing well. I plant my remaining eligible clippings. 

I notice biofilm forming at the surface of the water. There’s a lot of floating sediment now too. My friend points out wispy hair algae emerging from the leaves of my plants. I’m definitely doing a water change as soon as I can tomorrow.
