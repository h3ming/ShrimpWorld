There are now 14 salvinia leaves. 

SNAIL DISCOVERY !!!!!!!!!!!!!!! I found one in the moss! I discovered another smaller one later in the day. They are probably miniature ramshorn snails. I also saw some small white worms that Google and Reddit say are likely detritus worms. I'm extremely excited to see these signs of life in my tank. 

{% include slideshow.html photos=page.photos %}

I put in the order for a heater, thinking that since some of my plants come from tropical climates they might grow better in warming temperatures? Either way, I'm going to need it for the Chicago winter. 

The red stem plant in the middle seems to be doing well. I planted my remaining eligible clippings. 
Biofilm is beginning to form at the surface of the water. There’s a lot of floating sediment now too. My friend points out wispy hair algae emerging from the leaves of my plants. I’m definitely doing a water change as soon as I can tomorrow.
