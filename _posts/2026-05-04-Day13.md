There's a colony of around 10 shrimp that likes to stay on the right side of the tank. A couple reliable tank side crawlers. One floating plant surfer. 

{% include slideshow.html photos=page.tank %}

Snail population continues to grow. I spotted two new small ones today. There are at the very least 10 in the tank now, for that’s how much I could count with a cursory glance. They frequently graze the biofilm on the surface. 
Detritus worms feast on snail poop in the algae. A couple were on the surface too. 

A leaf came unplanted so I put it back. The dying(?) repens in the back is growing new leaves. I unblocked some of its light to give it a better chance.

The algae's become a network and foliage for my fauna. 

Floating plants continue to multiply. Roots are long and spiky.
