Colony of around 10 that likes to stay on the right side of the tank. A couple reliable tank side crawlers. One floating plant surfer. 
Snail population continues to grow. I spotted two new small ones today. There are at the very least 10 in the tank now, for that’s how much I could count with a cursory glance. They frequently graze the biofilm on the surface. 
Saw what looked like the detritus worms feasting on snail poop in the algae. A couple were on the surface too. 
A leaf came unplanted so I put it back. The repens in the back is growing leaves. I unblocked some of its light to give it a better chance.
Speaking of the algae, tufts of it are not visible in the front. It’s become a network and foliage for my fauna. 
Floating plants continue to multiply. Roots are long and spiky.
