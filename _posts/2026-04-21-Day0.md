I also tried a couple of tank planning/decorating software/webapps, but they were finicky and a bit difficult to use. 
I had a nice rock I knew I wanted as my main hardscape, so I started with that. I had picked it up in a river during a trip to Vancouver last summer with my family and brought it back with me to Chicago last fall with the intention of putting it in a shrimp ecosystem. Today, it fulfilled its destiny.
