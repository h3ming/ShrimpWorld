It’s 2:30pm and I’m driving up to Logan square for a rimless 5G tank. These things typically run for around $60, so I was very pleased with $35 I got it for. Snagging a parking spot by construction scaffolding of Ramen Wasabi, I wait for my contact to arrive. A black sedan pulls by a few minutes later, and the beautiful butch explains she had too many tanks already and apologizes for the cat hair, though I didn’t see any in the tank. She asks if I had been to the restaurant before and says it’s her favourite place in the city. “I’m going to walk in and pretend I’m in not coming in half an hour before closing” she says as a goodbye. 

I begin speaking to local aquatic plant sellers. I find someone in Lakeview, an uchicago alumni according to his profile, selling Pearlweed (a desired plant!). Though I can’t tell if he’s just saying it to be nice, I earnestly ask his recommendations for equipment and suppliers and he kindly humors me. We make plans to pick up on Sunday.
