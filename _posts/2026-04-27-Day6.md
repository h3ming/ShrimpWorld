Tested the water with the testing strips and was surprised to see some chlorine, so I put in a few more drops of the water conditioner. Small amount of nitrates but no nitrites.

I'm not sure how trustworthy these strips are. The pads bleed between one another sometimes and I really can't be sure how accurate the readings are. Supposedly the results are ready in 30 seconds, but the colours also change a lot over the course of 5 or so minutes. They were the much cheaper option though, and at least they're better than nothing.
