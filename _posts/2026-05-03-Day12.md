I tested the water again. 

##### Tank 
`GH - 9`  
`KH - 3/4`  

Still a bit high, but the KH is at a good level for them now. 

I read about how brita filtered water is not recommended becuase the filters contain silver which is toxic to shrimp. Guess I’ll buy some distilled water after all.
There’s a shrimp that likes to hang out on the floating plants. I watched a larger snail eat biofilm on the surface of the water. The algae has grown out enough from the front of the tank that there's new physical structure for the residents to move through. Evidence of this from snail poop tracks in the algae.
