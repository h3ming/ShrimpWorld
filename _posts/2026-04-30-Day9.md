Snipped some longer pearlweed stems and replanted them. Nitrates seemed to be a little high but they went down over the course of the day. The plants are doing their job. 

I try measuring the general hardness and carbonate hardness of water I have access to with a testing kit I ordered a few days ago. They come with chemicals and plastic 5mL tubes. To measure, one counts the amount of drops it takes to change the water to the target color; green for GH, yellow for KH. These are my readings.

#### Tank water
`GH: 9/10 161.1/179ppm`  
`KH: 4/5 71.6/89.5ppm`  
It’s a bit high.  
`pH: 7.5`

#### Tap
`GH: 9 161.1`  
`KH: 5 89.5`

#### Brita
`GH: 6 107.4`  
`KH: 3 53.7`

Crystal red shrimp, though lower is better, like a GH of up to 6 and a KH of up to 4. This means they can survive with my filtered Brita water! I couldn't see much discourse about using filtered drinking water online so I topped up the tank with my filtered water, about an inch. 

The plants fight for resources and grow. I increase the lighting period to 10 hours instead of 6. It's probably close to that now anyway, considering the amount of time I'm turning it on outside of its scheduling to look at the tank.
