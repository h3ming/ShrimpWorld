It's 3:45 pm. I'm in West Lakeview, saying goodbye to my [friend](https://www.instagram.com/coatsinkleaf/) and best of luck to his future as I head out from his first big gallery show in Chicago. Due to Visa issues under the current administration, he's being forced out of the country by this summer. If anyone reading this has ties to press/journalism and wants to help, his case for reapplication would strongly benefit from more published media. 

My pearlweed contact is only a 7 minute drive away. I notice a Menards right by his place, so I make a quick pit stop and successfully pick up some soil!!!! It's perfect: organic, non-fertilized, in a small enough quantity, and only about 3 bucks. What luck. 
I misjudge the amount of parking spots open by his place and accidentally park a block further than I need to and make my way to his building, where a small ziplock bag of pearlweed with my name on it is waiting in the foyer. With my large plant pickkup scheduled for the next day, I had asked for just 5 stems, but my pearlweed guy insisted that they were small and wouldn't give me under 10 for $5, and could do 20 for $7, so I ended up with the latter. 

Back home, I'm greeted with a number of packages. I unbox my orders of aquascaping tools, aquarium siphon, water testing strips, aquarium light, tap water conditioner, and gravel substrate. 

Things are coming together.
