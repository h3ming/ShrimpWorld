The algae continues to diminish, painting the centre foreground a dying wasteland. During its prime, the shrimp would swim/crawl as fast as they could through its tangled terrain. It felt something like a no-mans-land, a territory claimed by the algae. Both shrimp and snail never seemed fond of it.

Now, more of them are running up and taking snippets of the sludge, and eating them on the side.

I’ve identified the snails to be pink and pink leopard ramshorns (future tank master here -- they're actually golden, pink leopard, and blue leopard ramshorns).
