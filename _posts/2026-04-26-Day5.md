I saw a r/ChicagoAquaswap posting for some dwarf shrimp in the South Loop at a very good price. I reached out to the seller and learned they were moving and trying to get rid of everything by the end of the week. I told them I'd give them another message if my tank was cycled before then.

  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/firstlookshrimp.webp' | relative_url }}" alt="seller's picture of shrimp" class="inline-img">
  <figcaption>seller's picture of their shrimp</figcaption>
</figure>


I've been thinking about what kind of shrimp I wanted for the tank. I knew I was going to have neocaridina freshwater shrimp since they were generally hardier. Cherry shrimp are fan favourites, but I wanted something a bit more uncommon. I toyed with the idea of blue neocaridina shrimp, but sometimes their coloration tended towards black, which would make them difficult to stand out in the tank. I decided to go with red rili shrimp, which is what I thought the seller was selling, so I kept this option in mind.
