Today is a scouting day. I’m on the hunt for substrate. 
My first stop is Golden Aquarium in west Bridgeport. Going in, I’m greeted by the sound of trickling water and the largest set of fake breasts I have ever seen. The customer appears to be a regular, based on their friendly interaction with the shop owner. I comb through the available gravel and find one good option, but the colors are brighter than I prefer. One of the workers asks what I’m looking for, and I explain my predicament. The boss says there should be some gravel in the boxes by the entrance, and I walk around the store as they unpack their shipment. I study the plants for sale — someone of them are starting to look familiar — and take note of their shrimp and snail stock for the future. No blue shrimp at this location. The worker tells me they don’t have any more gravel in the size I want. 

I find black sand next door at the PetCo, but it’s too fine. The Ace hardware in the same strip only had large bags of soil and their buckets were unattractive. Back in my car, I put a 10lb bag of 2-4 mm aquarium gravel in “midnight pearl” in my Amazon cart. It’s really more of a brick colour overall, but I’m happy with it. 

Just because, I drive another 12 minutes to Home Depot, and the situation is dire. The soil options are either too big in quantity or the same as Target, except that they seem to come with a gnat problem. The buckets are nicer, but the intense orange would make it difficult to see clearly if I needed to. 
They didn’t have the lamp screw I was looking for outside of this project either. 

I end up getting a white bucket from the Ace hardware on in Hyde Park on my way back.

More money to Amazon.
