I've been watching the shrimp. The largest one seems to spend a lot of time still now or then grooming. Is it going to molt? I've read shrimp can stress molt from large changes to their water parameters. They seemed calm and normal entering the water last night, so perhaps this is not the case. I am still not confident about my water parameters and I hope they are doing well.
