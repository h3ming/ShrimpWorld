There’s been enough floating plant growth that more shrimp are comfortable hanging out up there. 
Snails have come to clean up the remaining fragments of the shrimp food. The rounded shells of several larger snails is making me suspect I have some bladder snails in my tank too. 
The repens are growing well behind the rock. The melting leaf of the crypt has quite a few holes in it today.
