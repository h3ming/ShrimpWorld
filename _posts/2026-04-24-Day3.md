I perform my first water change, about 30%. Pumping out the water uprooted my weak (ovalis?) in the back right corner, which I’ve been having trouble planting. With the lower water level, a pond-like smell becomes prominent. I scoop out the floating sediment best I can with a measuring cup and wipe away the dirt clinging to the sides of the tank. I snip and remove a dying leaf from the crypt. 5 snails now.
