Shrimp spotted eating a molt!!! This is a really good sign! I thought I had seen some molts the past few days but I wasn’t sure. This one is definitely a head.
This is very exciting. I’ve been stressing out about my water parameters and cycling, so this successful molt is huge. It means the tank conditions are good enough for them and as long as I keep things stable they should all continue to survive.

{% include slideshow.html photos=page.photo %}

Shrimp molts are the most dangerous times for them. Shedding their skin is the only way for shrimp to get larger, and the new shell is soft and vulnerable. Failed molts (getting stuck in their old shell) are fatal, and can be caused by imperfect water parameters. Since they were able to molt, they can survive :>. 

Next milestone is breeding…I want to see eggs….
