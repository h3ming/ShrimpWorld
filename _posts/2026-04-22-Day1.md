I'm using the Seaoura SR616 for lighting, as highly recommended by [Glass Box Diaries](https://glassboxdiaries.com/aquarium-accessories/). To prevent excessive early algae growth, I put it on a 6 hour timer (from 5pm to 11pm so I could enjoy the tank after class) for now. 
I couldn't remember if I performed the vinegar test on my rock at some point before putting it in. Sometimes the white band in river rocks is calcite, which would slowly dissolve and raise the pH of the tank. I googled a lot to see if my rock resembled any of the reactive rocks, but as I ultimately did not want to switch it out with anything I decided not to overthink it.
