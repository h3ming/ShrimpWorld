Slender metal pierces the surface of the world, plunging down with it a discus of pale red. The flesh-like saucer is injected just beneath the crest of the Great Mountain, escaping the notice of a lone mollusk scaling the rugged terrain. 
It remained undisturbed for a span, emitting a pungent-bloody odor which wafts through the water-air, screaming its location. Intrepid explorers soon approach. 
Hundreds of ommatidia cluster to compose the stalked compound eyes of the armored crustacean, millions of years of evolution priming the creature’s response to changes in light and movement. A six-man infantry of thread-like antennae wave about wildly as the many-legged scavenger scuttles up the speckled rock.
